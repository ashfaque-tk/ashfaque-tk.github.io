Consulting Offerings

Specialized Services & Mathematical Solutions

Helping companies transform messy transactional and inventory data into automated, cost-optimal operational decisions.

Probabilistic Demand Forecasting

Move beyond single-point estimates. I develop hierarchical, multi-horizon probabilistic models (P10, P50, P90) that account for seasonality, promotional uplifts, cannibalization, and intermittent SKU demand.

  • ✔ Hierarchical reconciliation across product trees & regions
  • ✔ Promotional & holiday feature engineering pipelines
  • ✔ Cold-start item forecasting with transfer learning & embeddings

Inventory & Multi-Echelon Policy Optimization

Formulating stochastic inventory policies $(s, S)$ and $(R, s, S)$ to balance working capital with strict service level targets under supplier lead-time uncertainty.

  • ✔ Dynamic safety stock sizing based on empirical lead-time variance
  • ✔ Multi-echelon inventory allocation (Central DC to local nodes)
  • ✔ MOQ (Minimum Order Quantity) and tiered bracket optimization

Operations Research & MILP Formulations

Custom Mixed-Integer Linear Programming (MILP) solvers for resource scheduling, production lot-sizing, facility location, and network flow optimization using Gurobi, PuLP, and Pyomo.

  • ✔ Rigorous mathematical formulations with explicit constraints
  • ✔ Scalable solver integration (Gurobi, HiGHS, CBC, OR-Tools)
  • ✔ Warm-starting and custom heuristic speed-ups for NP-hard models

End-to-End Decision Engines & APIs

Packaging optimization solvers and ML models into production-ready FastAPI microservices, containerized Docker deployments, and interactive decision dashboards for executive planning.
